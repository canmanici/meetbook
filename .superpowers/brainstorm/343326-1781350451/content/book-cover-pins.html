Pin Cluster Behavior

A

Expand Cluster on Tap

Tap a cluster (green circle with "3") → it zooms in and spreads out the individual book pins. Tap a pin → bottom sheet with book details.

B

Cluster → Mini List Sheet

Tap a cluster → opens a bottom sheet with a scrollable list of books in that area. Tap a book in the list → opens full book detail.
