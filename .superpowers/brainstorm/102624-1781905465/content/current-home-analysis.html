What's Working vs What's Not

✅ What Works

  • Clean card design with condition badges
  • Distance indicator is clear
  • Category filter chips
  • Search + ISBN scanner combo
  • List/Map toggle

❌ What's Missing

  • No greeting / personalization
  • No visual hero section
  • No "recently added" highlight
  • No neighborhood context
  • Flat — no visual hierarchy
  • No CTA for first-time users

Choose a Direction

Click to explore each design direction in detail

A

🟢 Warm Community Hub

Personalized greeting, "books near you" hero section with gradient overlay, neighborhood badge, horizontal scrollable featured books, then the full list. Warm, inviting, emphasizes community.

B

🟠 Card-First Discovery

Large hero book card (editor's pick or trending), stats bar (X books near you, Y users active), then a masonry/grid layout for browsing. More visual, less text-heavy, Instagram-meets-Pinterest feel.

C

🔵 Map-Native Experience

Map as the default view (not list), with a floating bottom sheet for book cards. Emphasizes the location-first nature of the app. Think Airbnb/Uber style. Bold, modern, distinct.

D

🟣 Hybrid Power User

Tabbed sections within the home: "For You" (personalized), "Nearby" (location), "Trending" (community). Combines best of all worlds. More complex but maximum engagement.
