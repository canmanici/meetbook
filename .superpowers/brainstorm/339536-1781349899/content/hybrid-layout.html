Toggle Placement Options

1

Floating Pill (Top-Right)

Toggle floats over the content area (like Google Maps layer button). Always visible, doesn't take layout space. Clean, modern.

2

Header Bar Segment

Toggle is part of the header bar, next to the title. More structured, less floating. Feels more like a tab switcher.

3

Bottom Floating Action

Toggle sits above the tab bar, like a FAB-style switcher. Thumb-friendly, always accessible without scrolling up.
